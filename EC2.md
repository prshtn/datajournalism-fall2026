## What the Data Shows!!

The FBI data shows that anti-Black or African American bias accounted for 15,245 reported hate crime incidents. Among the specifically named bias categories, anti-Black bias ranked highest, followed by anti-Jewish bias (7,732), anti-gay male bias (4,853), and anti-White bias (4,573). The largest overall category was "Other," with 24,801 incidents. Though the largest individual bias target was to Black and African-Americans.

**Source:** FBI Crime Data Explorer
