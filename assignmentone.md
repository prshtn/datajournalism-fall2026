# Story Pitch: Do Students Know What Their Health Center Offers?

**Interesting question:** How many students actually use the campus health center, and how much do students know about the services it provides?

For my final project, I want to look at whether students are taking advantage of the health center and whether a lack of awareness may be keeping some students from using it. The health center may offer more services than students realize, such as medical appointments, sexual health services, vaccinations, mental health referrals, or other resources.

**Potential data:** I could request information from the student health center about the number and types of appointments students use, while protecting patient privacy. I could also create my own dataset through an anonymous student survey asking students:

- Whether they have used the health center
- Which services they know are available
- Where they get health information
- Why they have or have not used the health center
- Whether they know how to make an appointment

**Why now:** Students are balancing academics, work, and other responsibilities, making convenient and affordable health care especially important. At the beginning of the academic year, students may also be unfamiliar with the resources available on campus. Understanding what students know—and what they don't—could reveal gaps in how the university communicates its health services.

**Potential sources:**

- Student health center director or staff
- University administrators
- Students who regularly use the health center
- Students who have never used it
- Student organizations focused on health and wellness
- Campus communications or marketing staff

The story could ultimately answer whether the health center is being fully utilized and whether students' perceptions of its services match what is actually available. I would also compare the university's promotion of these services with students' awareness of them.
